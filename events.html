Join us for our next event:

A Day of Making at Lighthaven, Summer Camp Edition!

On June 11th, we'll be hosting another Day of Making - this time at Summer Camp!

Come and spend the day making things in community at the best venue in town.

We'll have stations set up from 12-6 for you to try your hand at:

  • drawing/painting
  • sewing/mending
  • crocheting
  • leather working
  • embroidery
  • bookbinding
  • jewelry making
  • musicians, bring your instruments and jam!

Pull up and make something! If you're not sure where to start, you can come to one of the mini-workshops we have scheduled:

12:00 What is the Church of the First Mark? Grab your lunch and bring your questions. Come hear what we're all about. 12:30 Introduction to Bookbinding 1:00 Life Drawing 2:30 Introduction to Leather Working 3:00 How to Thread a Sewing Machine 3:00 Introduction to Jewelry Making 3:30 How to Crochet

Check the Summer Camp Waypoint app for locations. See you there!
