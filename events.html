Join us for our next event:

A Day of Making at Lighthaven!

On May 9th, we'll be hosting another Day of Making - this time in Berkeley at Lighthaven!

Come and spend the day making things in community at the best venue in town.

Here's our current schedule of mini-workshops for the day:

2:00 pm Doors open 3:00 pm Live model figure drawing 3:00 pm Make a Japanese stab binding notebook 4:00 pm Gathering 4:30 pm Writer's circle 4:30 pm Learn to crochet 4:30 pm Musician jam 6:00 pm Writer's critique circle 8:00 pm Musician jam (encore!)

Do you have a project you've been wanting to work on? Bring it!

Don't have a project yet? Don't worry, we will have so much for you to do!

We'll have stations set up all day for you to try all kinds of making

  • drawing/painting
  • sewing/mending
  • writers' corner
  • crocheting/knitting
  • leather working
  • embroidery
  • bookbinding
  • jewelry making
  • flower dying
  • musicians, bring your instruments and jam!

at least — we'll let you know as we add more!

Would you like to volunteer at the event? Please reach out to us at stewards@firstmarkchurch.org.

Lighthaven is generously giving us an incredible deal to use the space, but it isn't free. $20 covers the cost of the venue, and includes soft drinks and snacks. Additional donations to help cover the cost of materials are greatly appreciated. Kids 5 and under are free!

Already have your ticket? RSVP to the partiful invitation, and join the discord server to tell us what you'd like to make!
