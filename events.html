Church of the First Mark is a newly established church, devoted to the idea that making things, all sorts of things, is sacred. You can read more about us here.

This year we held three Days of Making — in March, in April, and in September at Lighthaven in Berkeley — with stations for knitting, sewing, painting, leatherworking, jewelry making, and more. We're planning what comes next.

The best way to hear about upcoming events first, and to talk with other makers in the meantime, is the church Discord. Come share what you're working on, ask for help when you're stuck, and tell us what you'd like to make at the next one.
