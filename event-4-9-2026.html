A Day of Making

April 9, 2026

Our first event was a great success - thanks to everyone who came out!

The format for this one will be a bit different. We'll break into smaller groups by Domain and work on more targeted projects. (You can find a description of Domains here.)

It might be a small group collaborating on one project, or several people making multiple versions of the same thing, or?

If you haven't already, join the Discord, check out the chatter in the various channels, see which one is resonating at the moment (you're never locked in to one Domain forever, just one at a time :), and chime in - what do you want to make?

Bring your friends, bring your projects, bring your ideas, and let's put new things into the world together.

Schedule:

Noon - doors open

12:30 pm - a short opening ritual

12:45 - Let the Making begin!

4:15 pm - We'll do a brief show and tell, share our thoughts about the experience, and close the service.

We are a nonprofit, and every donation helps us. If you can, a suggested donation of $15 is greatly appreciated.

Creation is hungry work! You're encouraged but not required to bring a drink or a snack to share. (Drinking can be helpful for getting the creative juices flowing, but guests who have had alcohol will not be allowed to use the power tools.)
